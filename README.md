The Meridian Job — a top-down stealth heist in vanilla JavaScript and canvas. Slip into the Meridian Gallery as Wren, a cat-burglar: cross the gallery floor under patrolling guards' vision cones and sweeping security cameras, lift the Aurora Diamond from its pedestal plus every loose treasure, toss decoy coins to pull guards off their routes, keep the alarm meter from filling, and get back to the exit before the house comes down on you.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD or arrows to move, Shift to tiptoe, click to toss a decoy coin
```
